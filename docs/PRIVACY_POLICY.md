## Overview

Deuteronomy 1 is a guitar training app. This policy describes exactly what data the app collects, stores, and shares — which is very little.

---

## What We Collect

### We do not collect personal information

Deuteronomy 1 does not require an account. We never collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personal identifier.

### Locally stored data only

All app data is stored on your device using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults). This includes:

- Your selected lesson mode and settings (e.g. starting fret, repetitions, skin preference)
- Purchased in-app unlocks (stored as UserDefaults flags)
- Bank balance (in-app currency, stored locally)

This data never leaves your device. We have no servers and no backend.

### No analytics SDKs

Deuteronomy 1 does **not** use Firebase, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, or any third-party analytics or crash reporting SDK. There is no telemetry, no usage tracking, and no data transmission of any kind.

### Audio

The app plays guitar notes and backing tracks using AVFoundation. It does **not** record audio, access the microphone, or process any audio input.

---

## What We Share

**Nothing.** We do not share, sell, rent, or transmit any data to any third party because we do not collect any data to share.

The only external service involved is the Apple App Store, which handles app distribution and any in-app purchases under Apple's own privacy policy.

---

## Children's Privacy

Deuteronomy 1 is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, there are no special concerns regarding users under 13. The app is fully COPPA-compliant by design.

---

## Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

- **View**: All settings are visible in the app
- **Change**: All settings can be modified in the app
- **Delete**: Deleting the app removes all locally stored data permanently

---

## International Users

We comply with GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable privacy regulations. Because we collect no personal data and operate no servers, the compliance burden is minimal by design.
